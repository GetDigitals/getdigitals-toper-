📖 Section A: Reading Comprehension (10 Marks)
Passage 1 5 Marks
The COVID-19 pandemic brought unprecedented changes to the education system worldwide. Schools and colleges were forced to shift to online modes of teaching overnight. While this transition ensured continuity of education, it also exposed the deep digital divide in society. Students from underprivileged backgrounds struggled to access smartphones and internet connections. Teachers, too, faced challenges in adapting to new technologies. However, the pandemic also accelerated the adoption of digital tools, making education more flexible and accessible in the long run.
Q1. What changes did COVID-19 bring to education? (2 Marks)
✅ Answer / Uttar
COVID-19 forced schools and colleges to shift to online modes of teaching overnight. It brought unprecedented changes, ensuring education continuity but also exposing the digital divide.
Q2. What problems did underprivileged students face? (2 Marks)
✅ Answer / Uttar
Underprivileged students struggled to access smartphones and internet connections. They could not participate in online classes due to lack of digital resources, widening the educational gap.
Q3. Find the word from the passage which means 'never done or known before'. (1 Mark)
✅ Answer / Uttar
Unprecedented — means never done or known before.
Passage 2 5 Marks
[Short Passage on "Benefits of Gardening"]

Gardening is not just a hobby; it is a therapeutic activity that benefits both mind and body. Spending time with plants reduces stress and anxiety. It provides physical exercise through digging, planting, and watering. Moreover, growing your own vegetables ensures fresh, organic produce for your family. Gardening also teaches patience and responsibility as plants need regular care and attention.
Q1. Why is gardening called a therapeutic activity? (2 Marks)
✅ Answer / Uttar
Gardening is called therapeutic because it reduces stress and anxiety. It benefits both mind and body, providing mental peace and physical exercise.
Q2. What values does gardening teach? (2 Marks)
✅ Answer / Uttar
Gardening teaches patience and responsibility. Plants need regular care and attention, which helps gardeners develop these important life values.

Q2. Analytical Paragraph 5 Marks
Write an analytical paragraph in 120 words on the given pie chart showing the monthly expenditure of a middle-class family.
✅ Answer / Uttar
The given pie chart illustrates the monthly expenditure pattern of a middle-class family. Food and groceries account for the largest share at 35%, reflecting the basic necessity. Housing and rent consume 25% of the income, which is significant in urban areas. Education takes up 15%, showing the family's priority towards children's schooling. Healthcare and transportation each account for 10%. The remaining 5% is spent on entertainment and miscellaneous expenses. Overall, the chart reveals that essential needs (food, housing, education) consume 75% of the income, leaving little room for savings. The family should consider reducing discretionary spending and exploring ways to increase savings for future security.
📚 Section C: Literature (20 Marks)
Extract 1: Glimpses of India 5 Marks
"The baker or pader used to come twice a day — once in the morning to sell his breads and once again after emptying his huge basket. The jingling thud of his bamboo woke us up from sleep and we ran to meet and greet him."
(a) Who is the 'pader' mentioned here? (1 Mark)
(b) How often did he come? (1 Mark)
(c) What sound woke the children up? (1 Mark)
(d) Why did the children run to meet him? (2 Marks)
✅ Answer / Uttar
(a) The 'pader' is the baker who sold bread in Goa.

(b) He used to come twice a day — once in the morning and once again after emptying his basket.

(c) The jingling thud of his bamboo (bamboo staff) woke the children up from sleep.

(d) The children ran to meet him because they were eager to buy bread (bolinhas). The baker was a friendly figure, and children loved the fresh bread he brought. His arrival was a happy event in their daily routine.
Extract 2: A Tiger in the Zoo 5 Marks
"He stalks in his vivid stripes
The few steps of his cage,
On pads of velvet quiet,
In his quiet rage."
(a) Who is 'he' in these lines? (1 Mark)
(b) What does 'vivid stripes' refer to? (1 Mark)
(c) Why is the tiger in 'quiet rage'? (2 Marks)
(d) What is the poetic device in 'pads of velvet quiet'? (1 Mark)
✅ Answer / Uttar
(a) 'He' refers to the tiger trapped in a zoo cage.

(b) 'Vivid stripes' refers to the bright, distinct black stripes on the tiger's orange body.

(c) The tiger is in 'quiet rage' because he is imprisoned in a small cage. His natural instinct is to hunt and roam freely in the jungle, but the cage restricts him. He is angry and frustrated but cannot express it violently, hence the 'quiet rage'.

(d) Metaphor — the tiger's soft paws are compared to 'pads of velvet'.
Short Answer: The Thief's Story 3 Marks
How did Anil's kindness transform Hari Singh?
✅ Answer / Uttar
Anil's kindness transformed Hari Singh in several ways:

1. Trust: Anil trusted Hari despite knowing he had no cooking skills. This trust made Hari feel valued.

2. Education: Anil taught Hari to read and write, giving him a skill that could lead to honest employment.

3. Moral Awakening: When Hari stole the money, he realized that Anil's kindness and trust were more precious than the stolen cash. He returned the money, choosing honesty over theft.

4. Second Chance: Anil's forgiving nature gave Hari a chance to reform and start a new life.
Long Answer: Bholi 5 Marks
How did Bholi's teacher transform her life? What does this story tell us about the power of education?
✅ Answer / Uttar
Teacher's Transformation of Bholi:
Bholi was a neglected, backward child — she had pockmarks on her face, stammered, and was considered a burden by her family. Her teacher at the village school changed her life completely:

1. Confidence Building: The teacher spoke to Bholi kindly and encouraged her. She told Bholi that with practice, she could speak without stammering.

2. Education: The teacher taught Bholi to read and write. Over the years, Bholi became knowledgeable and confident.

3. Self-Respect: The teacher instilled self-respect in Bholi. When Bishamber, the lame old groom, demanded dowry because of Bholi's pockmarks, Bholi refused to marry him — a bold step she could never have taken without education.

4. Independence: Bholi decided to become a teacher herself and serve the village school, choosing independence over a demeaning marriage.

Power of Education:
The story demonstrates that education is the most powerful tool for transformation. It can turn a timid, neglected girl into a confident, self-respecting woman. Education gives voice to the voiceless and courage to the oppressed. Bholi's transformation from 'dumb cow' to 'Sulekha' shows that with proper guidance and education, anyone can overcome their circumstances and achieve dignity.
Short Answer: Glimpses of India 2 Marks
Why did the children enjoy the baker's visits so much?
✅ Answer / Uttar
The children enjoyed the baker's visits because they got fresh bread and bolinhas (bread bangles), and his daily arrival, announced by the jingling thud of his bamboo staff, was an exciting, joyful part of their routine that they eagerly looked forward to.
