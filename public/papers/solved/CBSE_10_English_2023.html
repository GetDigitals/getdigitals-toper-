Q1. Formal Letter 5 Marks
You are Aman/Anita of 23, Rajendra Nagar, Kanpur. Write a letter to the Editor of a newspaper about the increasing incidents of road accidents due to rash driving and suggesting measures to prevent them.
✅ Answer / Uttar
23, Rajendra Nagar,
Kanpur - 208001

The Editor,
The Hindustan Times,
Kanpur

Subject: Increasing Road Accidents Due to Rash Driving

Sir/Madam,

Through the columns of your esteemed newspaper, I wish to draw the attention of the authorities and the public towards the alarming increase in road accidents in our city due to rash and negligent driving.

Every day, newspapers report tragic accidents caused by speeding vehicles, drunk driving, and violation of traffic rules. Youngsters often ride bikes without helmets, and truck drivers flout speed limits. Pedestrians are the worst victims, with many losing their lives on zebra crossings.

To curb this menace, I suggest the following measures:

1. Strict enforcement of traffic rules and heavy fines for violators
2. Installation of CCTV cameras and speed guns at accident-prone areas
3. Regular awareness campaigns in schools and colleges
4. Mandatory helmet and seatbelt rules
5. Decongestion of roads and proper maintenance of traffic signals

I urge the traffic police and municipal authorities to take immediate action before more innocent lives are lost.

Yours faithfully,
Aman Gupta
Q2. Analytical Paragraph 5 Marks
Write an analytical paragraph in 120 words on the given line graph showing the rising trend of online shopping among teenagers from 2018 to 2023.
✅ Answer / Uttar
The given line graph depicts the growing trend of online shopping among teenagers from 2018 to 2023. In 2018, only 20% of teenagers engaged in online shopping. This figure rose steadily to 35% in 2019 and 50% in 2020, likely influenced by the COVID-19 pandemic and lockdowns. The upward trend continued, reaching 65% in 2021, 78% in 2022, and peaking at 85% in 2023. This dramatic increase can be attributed to smartphone accessibility, attractive discounts, and the convenience of home delivery. However, this trend raises concerns about impulse buying, reduced physical activity, and cybersecurity risks among teenagers. Parents and educators should guide teenagers to shop responsibly and protect their personal information online.
📝 Section B (contd.): Grammar (10 Marks) — Writing + Grammar = 20 Marks total
Q1. Gap Filling 3 Marks
(a) She ______ (wait) for the bus for over an hour.
(b) By next year, I ______ (complete) my graduation.
(c) If she ______ (study) harder, she would have passed.
✅ Answer / Uttar
(a) She has been waiting for the bus for over an hour. (Present Perfect Continuous — 'for' + duration, action continues)
(b) By next year, I will have completed my graduation. (Future Perfect — action completed by a future time)
(c) If she had studied harder, she would have passed. (Third conditional — past perfect in if-clause)

Q3. Reported Speech 4 Marks
(a) She said, "I am going to Mumbai tomorrow."
(b) He said to me, "Do you know the way?"
(c) The doctor said, "Take this medicine twice a day."
(d) Ravi said, "Alas! I have lost my wallet."
✅ Answer / Uttar
(a) She said that she was going to Mumbai the next day. (Present continuous → past continuous, tomorrow → next day)
(b) He asked me if I knew the way. (Yes/No question → if + past tense)
(c) The doctor advised me to take that medicine twice a day. (Imperative → advised + to + verb, this → that)
(d) Ravi exclaimed with sorrow that he had lost his wallet. (Exclamatory → exclaimed with sorrow, alas removed)
📚 Section C: Literature (40 Marks)
Extract 1: Dust of Snow 5 Marks
"The way a crow
Shook down on me
The dust of snow
From a hemlock tree"
(a) What did the crow shake down on the poet? (1 Mark)
(b) What is a 'hemlock tree'? Why has the poet mentioned it? (2 Marks)
(c) What change did this incident bring in the poet's mood? (2 Marks)
✅ Answer / Uttar
(a) The crow shook down the dust of snow on the poet.

(b) A hemlock tree is a poisonous tree with white flowers. The poet mentions it because: (i) It symbolizes sorrow and depression (as hemlock is poisonous), and (ii) It contrasts with the joyful, unexpected moment of the crow shaking snow, showing that beauty can be found even in dark moments.

(c) The dust of snow falling on the poet changed his mood. It made him feel happier and saved some part of his day from being wasted in regret or sorrow. The small, unexpected moment of nature lifted his spirits.
Extract 2: Madam Rides the Bus 5 Marks
"Valli!" said her mother. "What's the matter with you? Why are you smiling to yourself? Keep your eyes on the road, or you'll get hurt."
(a) Why was Valli smiling to herself? (2 Marks)
(b) What does this extract tell us about Valli's mother? (2 Marks)
(c) What had Valli just experienced? (1 Mark)
✅ Answer / Uttar
(a) Valli was smiling to herself because she was remembering her first bus ride — the exciting experience of travelling alone, seeing new sights, and feeling independent. The memory brought her joy.

(b) Valli's mother is caring but unaware of Valli's secret adventure. She is concerned about Valli's safety ("Keep your eyes on the road") but doesn't understand her daughter's inner world and dreams.

(c) Valli had just experienced her first solo bus ride from her village to the nearby town and back — a secret adventure she had planned and saved for.

Long Answer: How to Tell Wild Animals 5 Marks
How does the poet Carolyn Wells describe different wild animals in a humorous way? What is the tone of the poem?
✅ Answer / Uttar
Humorous Descriptions:
1. Asian Lion: The poet says if you see a large, tawny beast roaring at you in the jungle, you'll know it's an Asian Lion — "if he roars at you as you're dying, you'll know it is the Asian Lion." The humour lies in the casual, almost absurd observation.

2. Bengal Tiger: Described as a noble beast with black stripes on yellow skin who jumps on you and eats you. The humour is in the matter-of-fact way the poet describes a life-threatening situation.

3. Leopard: The poet says if you see spots and it jumps on you repeatedly ("lep... lep again"), it's a leopard. The playful repetition adds humour.

4. Bear: The bear hugs you tightly — "A novice might nonplus — the bear will hug you very hard."

5. Crocodile vs Hyena: The crocodile weeps (crocodile tears) while the hyena smiles — both are dangerous but show false emotions.

6. Chameleon: The poet says you won't see it on a tree because it changes colour and blends in — a witty observation about camouflage.

Tone: The tone is light-hearted, humorous, and playful. The poet uses irony, exaggeration, and wordplay to make a potentially scary topic (wild animals) entertaining and memorable.
Long Answer: The Necklace 5 Marks
How did one evening at the ball change Mathilde Loisel's life? What does this story teach us about contentment?
✅ Answer / Uttar
How the Ball Changed Mathilde's Life:
Mathilde Loisel, a middle-class woman, borrowed a diamond necklace from her friend Madame Forestier to wear at a minister's ball. She was the centre of attention and felt like a queen for one night. However, while returning home, she lost the necklace.

To replace it, she and her husband borrowed 36,000 francs and bought an identical necklace. This debt forced them into ten years of gruelling poverty. They moved to a smaller apartment, dismissed their maid, and Mathilde did all household work herself. Her beauty faded, and she became a coarse, hard woman.

The cruel irony was revealed at the end — the original necklace was fake, worth only 500 francs. Mathilde had sacrificed her youth and beauty for nothing.

Lesson about Contentment:
The story teaches that discontentment and vanity lead to destruction. Mathilde was never happy with her comfortable life; she always wanted more. Her desire to appear rich cost her everything. The story warns us to value what we have and not be driven by materialistic desires and social pretensions. True happiness comes from accepting one's circumstances, not from artificial displays of wealth.

Long Answer: For Anne Gregory (Poem) 5 Marks
What is the central message of the poem "For Anne Gregory" by W.B. Yeats? How does the poet distinguish between outer beauty and inner self?
✅ Answer / Uttar
Central Message:
The poem conveys that true, lasting love is for a person's inner self, not just physical beauty. The poet argues that people are often drawn to outward appearance — in this case, Anne's beautiful yellow hair — rather than her true character.

Outer Beauty vs Inner Self:
The poet tells Anne that young men love her only for her "yellow hair" (a symbol of physical beauty), not for herself. He suggests that only God could love her for herself alone and not her yellow hair, implying that among humans, superficial attraction to appearance is nearly universal and hard to escape. The poem gently criticizes society's obsession with outer beauty over inner worth.
Short Answer: The Trees (Poem) 4 Marks
What does the poem "The Trees" by Adrienne Rich symbolize?
✅ Answer / Uttar
The poem symbolizes the forest's silent departure from the confines of a house — representing nature's struggle for freedom and self-assertion against restriction. The trees moving out into the forest at night can be read as a metaphor for reclaiming natural rights and independence, and by extension, for any suppressed or restrained force in the world quietly awakening and asserting itself.
