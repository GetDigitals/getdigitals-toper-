Passage 1: Sustainable Development 10 Marks
Sustainable development means meeting the needs of the present without compromising the ability of future generations to meet their own needs. As the world grapples with climate change, resource depletion, and pollution, the concept of sustainability has moved from an environmental buzzword to an economic necessity. Governments and industries are now investing in renewable energy, waste management, and eco-friendly technologies. However, sustainable development is not just the government's responsibility — individual choices like reducing plastic use, conserving water, and supporting local produce also contribute significantly to a greener future.
Q1. What is sustainable development? (2 Marks)
✅ Answer / Uttar
Sustainable development means meeting the needs of the present without compromising the ability of future generations to meet their own needs — balancing growth today with resource availability for tomorrow.
Q2. Why has sustainability become an economic necessity? (2 Marks)
✅ Answer / Uttar
Sustainability has become an economic necessity because the world is facing climate change, resource depletion, and pollution — issues that directly threaten long-term economic stability, forcing governments and industries to act.
Q3. Find the word from the passage which means 'the reduction of a resource'. (1 Mark)
✅ Answer / Uttar
Depletion — means the reduction or exhaustion of a resource.
Q4. How can individuals contribute to sustainable development? (3 Marks)
✅ Answer / Uttar
Individuals can contribute by reducing plastic use, conserving water, and supporting local produce. The passage emphasizes that sustainability is not the government's responsibility alone — everyday personal choices add up to make a real difference.
Q5. What are governments and industries investing in? (2 Marks)
✅ Answer / Uttar
They are investing in renewable energy, waste management, and eco-friendly technologies to address environmental challenges.

3. Install adequate dustbins at key points in the locality

Aman Kumar

Q1. Gap Filling (Tenses) 3 Marks
Fill in the blanks with the correct form of verbs given in brackets:
(a) They ______ (live) in this city for ten years.
(b) By the time the fire brigade arrived, the fire ______ (already/spread).
(c) If she ______ (study) harder, she would pass the exam.
✅ Answer / Uttar
(a) They have been living in this city for ten years. (Present Perfect Continuous — action started in past, continues)
(b) By the time the fire brigade arrived, the fire had already spread. (Past Perfect — completed before another past action)
(c) If she studied harder, she would pass the exam. (Second conditional — hypothetical present/future)

Q3. Reported Speech 4 Marks
Change the following into indirect speech:
(a) The scientist said, "Water boils at 100 degrees Celsius."
(b) She said to me, "Where do you live?"
(c) He said, "I am going to Mumbai tomorrow."
(d) The officer said, "Stand still!"
✅ Answer / Uttar
(a) The scientist said that water boils at 100 degrees Celsius. (Scientific fact — tense unchanged)
(b) She asked me where I lived. (Wh-question — question word retained, tense shifts back)
(c) He said that he was going to Mumbai the next day. (am going → was going, tomorrow → the next day)
(d) The officer ordered him/her to stand still. (Imperative — ordered + to + verb)
📚 Section C: Literature (40 Marks)

Literature section mein First Flight aur Footprints Without Feet dono se extract-based, short answer aur long answer questions aate hain.

Extract 1: From the Diary of Anne Frank 5 Marks
"I don't want to jot down a series of bald facts in a diary as most people do, but I want this diary itself to be my friend, and I shall call my friend Kitty."
(a) Who is the speaker, and what is 'Kitty'? (2 Marks)
(b) Why did Anne want a diary to be her friend? (2 Marks)
(c) What kind of diary did Anne NOT want to write? (1 Mark)
✅ Answer / Uttar
(a) The speaker is Anne Frank. 'Kitty' is the name she gave to her diary, treating it as a close, personal friend.

(b) Anne wanted the diary to be her friend because she felt she had no true confidant among her friends or family with whom she could share her deepest thoughts and feelings freely.

(c) Anne did NOT want to write a series of bald (plain, factual) facts — she wanted her diary to reflect genuine emotion and personal connection, not a dry record of events.
Extract 2: Fire and Ice (Poem) 5 Marks
"Some say the world will end in fire,
Some say in ice.
From what I've tasted of desire
I hold with those who favor fire."
(a) What do 'fire' and 'ice' symbolize in the poem? (2 Marks)
(b) Why does the poet favour 'fire'? (2 Marks)
(c) Name the poet of this poem. (1 Mark)
✅ Answer / Uttar
(a) 'Fire' symbolizes desire and passion (destructive emotions like greed and lust), while 'ice' symbolizes hatred and indifference (cold, cruel emotions).

(b) The poet favours 'fire' because he has personally experienced the destructive power of desire, and believes it could destroy the world just as effectively as hatred could.

(c) The poem was written by Robert Frost.
Short Answer: A Question of Trust 3 Marks
How did Horace Danby's love for rare books lead him into trouble?
✅ Answer / Uttar
Horace Danby's love for rare books drove him to commit safe-breaking robberies once a year to fund his secret hobby of collecting rare, expensive books — something his legitimate income as a locksmith couldn't afford. This illegal habit eventually led him into a house where he was outwitted and tricked by a woman posing as the owner, resulting in his own downfall.
Long Answer: The Proposal 5 Marks
How does Anton Chekhov use humour to present the theme of marriage and property in "The Proposal"?
✅ Answer / Uttar
Humour Through Absurdity:
Chekhov presents Ivan Lomov's marriage proposal to Natalya as a farce — instead of romance, the couple ends up in fierce arguments over property (the Oxen Meadows) and whose dog is better (Squeezer vs Guess), completely forgetting the actual purpose of the visit.

Theme of Marriage and Property:
The play satirizes how in that society, marriage was often more about property and social alliance than love. Both families were more concerned with land and material possessions than genuine affection, highlighted by how quickly the quarrels escalate and how easily they're forgotten once the proposal is 'accepted'.

Conclusion:
The comic irony lies in the fact that Lomov and Natalya, both quarrelsome and hypochondriac, are shown as a perfectly matched (if absurd) couple, poking fun at superficial reasons for marriage in their social class.
Long Answer: Bholi 5 Marks
What role did education play in transforming Bholi's self-image and life decisions?
✅ Answer / Uttar
Bholi's Transformation:
Bholi, once considered dull, disfigured by smallpox, and a burden to her family, was transformed by education into a confident, self-respecting young woman. Her teacher's kindness and patience helped her overcome her stammer and build self-esteem.

Impact on Life Decisions:
Education gave Bholi the courage to reject a demeaning marriage to Bishamber, an old, greedy man who insulted her at the wedding over dowry. Instead, she chose to become a teacher herself, dedicating her life to educating other underprivileged children.

Message:
The story shows that education is empowering — it doesn't just impart knowledge but builds the confidence and self-worth needed to make bold, independent life choices.
Extract 3: The Midnight Visitor 5 Marks
"Ausable was fat... Ausable was, unlike the fictional secret agents, immensely fat. And the woman on the balcony... there wasn't a balcony."
(a) Who is Ausable, and how is he different from typical spy characters? (2 Marks)
(b) What lie did Ausable tell Max about the balcony? (2 Marks)
(c) What was Max's profession? (1 Mark)
✅ Answer / Uttar
(a) Ausable is a secret agent, but unlike fictional spies who are typically slim, dashing, and dramatic, he is fat, calm, and unremarkable-looking — his intelligence and quick thinking, not physical prowess, make him effective.

(b) Ausable told Max that there was no real balcony outside the window — that it was a trap set by the French police to catch spies, and stepping onto it would trigger an alarm.

(c) Max was a spy/thief attempting to steal a secret report from Ausable.
Short Answer: For Anne Gregory (Poem) 3 Marks
What does the poet suggest is the only way Anne can be loved for herself?
✅ Answer / Uttar
The poet suggests that only God could love Anne for herself alone, not for her yellow hair (outer beauty) — implying that among ordinary people, physical appearance almost always influences love, and true unconditional love for one's inner self is a near-impossible, almost divine ideal.
Long Answer: The Making of a Scientist 5 Marks
What qualities helped Richard Ebright become a successful scientist? How did his curiosity shape his journey?
✅ Answer / Uttar
Key Qualities:
Richard Ebright's success stemmed from his intense curiosity, perseverance, and love for research from a very young age. He began by collecting butterflies as a child, guided by his mother, and this hobby gradually evolved into serious scientific inquiry.

Shaping Journey:
His curiosity led him to investigate the viral cause of a disease affecting monarch butterfly caterpillars, and later to conduct groundbreaking research on how cells read genes, eventually developing a new theory on cell receptors that was published while he was still a student.

Conclusion:
The chapter shows that curiosity combined with disciplined, sustained effort — not just natural talent — is what transforms a child's hobby into genuine scientific achievement.
Short Answer: The Ball Poem 4 Marks
What is the significance of the line "Money is external" in "The Ball Poem"?
✅ Answer / Uttar
The line "money is external" means that while a new ball can easily be bought with money, it can never replace the emotional attachment and personal history tied to the ball that was lost. The poet emphasizes that some losses are irreplaceable regardless of material compensation, and that true growing up involves learning to accept and internalize such losses rather than simply substituting them.
