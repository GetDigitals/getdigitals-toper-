Prove that √5 is an irrational number.

✅ Solution:
Let √5 be rational. Then √5 = p/q where p, q are co-prime integers. Squaring: 5 = p²/q² ⇒ p² = 5q² ∴ p² is divisible by 5 ⇒ p is divisible by 5 Let p = 5k (5k)² = 5q² ⇒ 25k² = 5q² ⇒ q² = 5k² ∴ q² is divisible by 5 ⇒ q is divisible by 5 This contradicts that p and q are co-prime. ∴ √5 is irrational. (Proved)

A motor boat whose speed is 24 km/h in still water takes 1 hour more to go 32 km upstream than to return downstream to the same spot. Find the speed of the stream.

✅ Solution:
Let speed of stream = x km/h Speed upstream = (24 – x) km/h Speed downstream = (24 + x) km/h Time upstream = 32/(24–x) Time downstream = 32/(24+x) Given: 32/(24–x) – 32/(24+x) = 1 32[(24+x) – (24–x)] / [(24–x)(24+x)] = 1 32(2x) / (576 – x²) = 1 64x = 576 – x² x² + 64x – 576 = 0 x² + 72x – 8x – 576 = 0 x(x + 72) – 8(x + 72) = 0 (x – 8)(x + 72) = 0 x = 8 or x = –72 (rejected) ∴ Speed of stream = 8 km/h

Prove that in a right triangle, the square on the hypotenuse is equal to the sum of the squares on the other two sides.
OR
In ΔABC, if AD is the median, then prove that AB² + AC² = 2(AD² + BD²).

✅ Solution (Apollonius Theorem):
Given: ΔABC with median AD. To Prove: AB² + AC² = 2(AD² + BD²) Construction: Draw AE ⊥ BC. Proof: In right ΔAEB: AB² = AE² + BE² = AE² + (BD + DE)² = AE² + BD² + DE² + 2BD·DE In right ΔAEC: AC² = AE² + EC² = AE² + (CD – DE)² = AE² + CD² + DE² – 2CD·DE Since BD = CD (AD is median): AB² + AC² = 2AE² + 2BD² + 2DE² + 2BD·DE – 2BD·DE = 2(AE² + DE²) + 2BD² = 2AD² + 2BD² = 2(AD² + BD²) Hence Proved.

The angles of depression of the top and bottom of an 8 m tall building from the top of a multi-storeyed building are 30° and 45° respectively. Find the height of the multi-storeyed building and the distance between the two buildings. (Use √3 = 1.732)

✅ Solution:
Let multi-storeyed building = AB = h m Small building = CD = 8 m Distance between buildings = BD = x m In right ΔACE (where E is on AB such that CE || BD): AE = AB – BE = h – 8 In right ΔACE, angle of depression to C = 30°: tan 30° = AE/CE = (h–8)/x 1/√3 = (h–8)/x x = (h–8)√3 ... (i) In right ΔABD, angle of depression to D = 45°: tan 45° = AB/BD = h/x 1 = h/x x = h ... (ii) From (i) and (ii): h = (h–8)√3 h = h√3 – 8√3 h(√3 – 1) = 8√3 h = 8√3/(√3 – 1) = 8√3(√3 + 1)/[(√3–1)(√3+1)] = 8√3(√3 + 1)/(3–1) = 8√3(√3 + 1)/2 = 4√3(√3 + 1) = 4(3 + √3) = 12 + 4√3 = 12 + 4(1.732) = 12 + 6.928 = 18.928 m Distance x = h = 18.928 m ≈ 18.93 m ∴ Height of multi-storeyed building = 18.93 m Distance between buildings = 18.93 m
