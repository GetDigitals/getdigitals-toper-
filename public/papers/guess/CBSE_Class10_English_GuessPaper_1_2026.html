Discursive Passage (10 marks)
Discipline is the bridge between goals and accomplishment. It is not merely about following rules set by others, but about self-control and the ability to regulate one's own behaviour. In the life of a student, discipline plays a pivotal role. A disciplined student manages time effectively, completes assignments on schedule, and maintains a healthy balance between studies and extracurricular activities. However, discipline should not be confused with rigidity. True discipline comes from within — it is self-motivation rather than external pressure. When students understand the 'why' behind their actions, they are more likely to stay committed. For instance, a student who understands that regular exercise improves concentration will exercise willingly, not because a parent forces them, but because they see the benefit. In today's digital age, discipline has become even more critical. With smartphones and social media constantly vying for attention, the ability to focus has become a rare skill. Students who can resist the temptation to check their phones every few minutes tend to perform better academically. Research shows that multitasking reduces productivity by up to 40%, making disciplined focus a valuable asset. Parents and teachers can foster discipline by setting clear expectations, providing consistent routines, and most importantly, leading by example. When adults demonstrate disciplined behaviour — reading instead of scrolling, completing tasks on time, managing emotions — children naturally absorb these habits. The goal is not to create robots but to help young minds develop the internal compass that guides them toward success.

Q7(a)3 marks
What does Mandela mean when he says that freedom is indivisible? (40-50 words)
Answer: Mandela means freedom cannot be partial. If one person is not free, no one is truly free. The oppressor is also unfree, imprisoned by hatred and prejudice. True freedom comes when everyone — oppressed and oppressor alike — is liberated.Mandela kehna chahte hain ki freedom ko baata nahi ja sakta. Jab tak ek bhi aadmi gulaam hai, koi bhi truly free nahi hai.
Q7(b)3 marks
How did the seagull's parents teach him to fly? What does this tell us about their parenting style? (40-50 words)
Answer: The parents used 'tough love' — they starved him, taunted him for cowardice, and threatened to leave him alone. His mother even tempted him with food while flying. This shows a parenting style that was challenging but ultimately caring, pushing the child out of his comfort zone.Parents ne tough love use ki — bhookha rakha, taunt kiya. Yeh parenting style challenging tha par caring bhi, jo bachche ko comfort zone se bahar nikaalta hai.
Q7(c)3 marks
Why did Anne Frank consider her diary to be her true friend? (40-50 words)
Answer: Anne considered her diary her true friend because she could share her deepest thoughts and fears without being judged. Unlike human friends, the diary never betrayed or misunderstood her — it was always there to listen during her lonely days of hiding.Anne ko diary sachcha dost laga kyunki woh usse bina judge kiye sab kuch share kar sakti thi, especially chhupne ke dino mein.
Q7(d)3 marks
How did the dust of snow change the poet's mood? What message does the poem convey? (40-50 words)
Answer: Dust of snow from a hemlock tree, shaken by a crow, fell on the poet and changed his sad mood into a happy one. The poem conveys that small, unexpected moments of nature can bring great joy and change our perspective on life.Crow ne hemlock tree se snow gira di jo poet pe pada aur uska mood badal gaya. Poem sikhata hai ki chhoti natural moments bhi badi khushi laa sakti hain.
Q7(e)3 marks
Why does Amanda wish to be an orphan? What does this reveal about her state of mind? (40-50 words)
Answer: Amanda wishes to be an orphan because she feels constantly nagged and controlled by her parents. She imagines being free to roam and live peacefully without instructions. This reveals her desire for freedom and frustration with parental control.Amanda orphan banana chahti hai kyunki parents usse hamesha kuch na kuch kehte rehte hain. Yeh uski freedom ki chaahat aur frustration dikhata hai.
QBonus
According to Robert Frost, what will cause the end of the world — fire or ice? Explain with reference to human emotions. (40-50 words)
Answer: Frost says both fire (desire) and ice (hatred) can destroy the world. Fire represents uncontrolled desire and greed; ice represents cold hatred and indifference. Both, taken to extremes, are equally capable of bringing destruction.Fire = desire/greed; Ice = hatred/indifference. Dono emotions extreme ho toh duniya barbaad kar sakte hain.

Long Answer Question — First Flight (6 marks)
Attempt any 1 out of the following 2 questions. 100-120 words. 6 marks.
Q9(a)6 marks
"Faith in God can move mountains." Do you agree with this statement in the context of Lencho's story? (100-120 words)
Answer: Yes, I strongly agree that faith in God can move mountains, as illustrated in Lencho's story. Lencho was a simple farmer who had complete, unshakeable faith in God. When a hailstorm destroyed his crop, he didn't lose hope — he wrote a letter to God asking for 100 pesos to sow his field again. Although the post office employees collected money to help him, Lencho's faith never wavered. Even when he received only 70 pesos, he doubted the post office employees, never God. This story teaches us that true faith gives us the strength to face any adversity. Lencho's unwavering belief inspires us that trusting completely gives us courage to overcome life's biggest challenges.Lencho ki kahani sikhati hai ki sachcha vishwas insaan ko mushkil se ladne ki taqat deta hai. Usne God pe poora bharosa rakha aur ummeed nahi chhodi.
Q9(b)6 marks
Describe Nelson Mandela's concept of 'twin obligations'. How did he fulfil both? (100-120 words)
Answer: Mandela speaks of a 'twin obligation' — the duty a person owes to their family and the duty owed to their people/country. For most people these obligations don't conflict, but for a man in Mandela's position, fighting for his country's freedom meant sacrificing his family life. He spent 27 years in prison, away from his wife and children, prioritising the freedom of his people. Mandela fulfilled his obligation to his country by dedicating his life to ending apartheid, and though it cost him personal family time, he believed a free country was the greater gift he could give his family and all South Africans, oppressor and oppressed alike.Mandela 'twin obligation' ki baat karte hain — family ke prati aur desh ke prati duty. Unhone desh ki azaadi ke liye family life qurban ki, 27 saal jail mein rahe.

Q10(a)6 marks
How did education transform Bholi's life? What does her story tell us about the importance of education for girls? (100-120 words)
Answer: Education completely transformed Bholi's life. Initially, she was a neglected girl who stammered and was called 'dumb' by everyone. Her parents were ready to marry her to an old, greedy man who demanded dowry. However, after going to school, Bholi gained confidence — her teacher's kindness helped her overcome her stammering, and she learned to read and write. Education gave her the courage to stand up against injustice: when Bishamber demanded dowry, Bholi firmly refused to marry him, surprising everyone with her newfound confidence. Her story powerfully illustrates that education is the most powerful weapon for girls, giving them self-respect, independence, and the ability to make their own decisions.Bholi school jaane se pehle darpok, stammering wali ladki thi. Education ne usse confident banaya. Dowry ke liye shaadi mana karke usne sabko hairaan kar diya.
Q10(b)6 marks
How does the story 'A Question of Trust' highlight the irony of a thief being outwitted? (100-120 words)
Answer: Horace Danby, a respected and 'law-abiding' man except for his secret hobby of safe-breaking, plans to rob a rich woman's safe. He is tricked by a young woman posing as the homeowner's niece, who convinces him to hand over the jewels and money he had collected, promising to return with the combination that never comes. The irony is sharp: an experienced, careful thief who prided himself on never getting caught is completely outwitted by an even cleverer thief pretending to be an innocent victim. He is arrested afterward for a crime he did not even complete — the jewels were fake, planted by the police.Horace Danby ek chor hai jo ek aurat ka safe todne jaata hai, par ek chalak ladki usse hi dhoka de deti hai. Irony yeh hai ki ek experienced chor khud thag liya jaata hai.
